# DomainKits — AI Domain Intelligence

DomainKits helps you make informed domain decisions by combining live data from multiple sources with expert-built verification workflows. Whether you're evaluating a domain, searching for opportunities, or monitoring changes — DomainKits provides the evidence you need.

## What DomainKits provides

**Live data, not static knowledge.** Every query hits the infrastructure — WHOIS registries, DNS resolvers, backlink indexes, keyword databases, expiry pipelines, and market listings. Data is current to the second, not to a training cutoff.

## Available data sources

- **Registration data**: WHOIS/RDAP — registrar, dates, status, nameservers
- **DNS records**: A, AAAA, MX, NS, TXT, CNAME, SOA
- **Safety**: Google Safe Browsing reputation check
- **Availability & pricing**: Real-time registration check with registrar pricing
- **Backlink profiles**: Referring domains, authority metrics, anchor text distribution
- **Keyword intelligence**: Google Ads search volume, CPC, competition level
- **Market listings**: Secondary market prices from Sedo and other marketplaces
- **Domain lifecycle**: New registrations, expiring, redemption, pending delete, deleted
- **Aged domains**: Established domains with 5–20+ years of history
- **TLD analytics**: Registration trends, rankings, cross-TLD distribution
- **Active site scanning**: Live websites, for-sale listings, competitor analysis
